Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

This policy explains the information the Video Saver Chrome extension handles to detect compatible HLS video and save the quality selected by the user as an MP4.

Information handled

Video Saver handles the current page URL and title, HLS playlist and media-resource URLs, limited player metadata such as the displayed video name and available qualities, selected media bytes, and the current download status and diagnostic log.

If a user activates Video Saver Pro, the extension also handles the license key entered by the user, a randomly generated installation identifier, the purchased plan and product/price IDs, license status, expiry when applicable, and license-validation timestamps.

How the information is used

This information is used only to detect the video requested by the user, retrieve its HLS playlists and segments from the source website or its media CDN, assemble compatible media locally into an MP4, show progress, provide the purchased entitlement, and troubleshoot a failed download.

Storage and retention

Download state and diagnostic information are stored locally in Chrome storage so progress remains available when the popup is closed. Job information is removed when the related browser tab closes or Chrome restarts. The number and time of successful downloads are stored locally to enforce the disclosed free allowance.

For Pro users, the license key, installation identifier, plan, product/price IDs, status, expiry, and validation timestamps are stored in chrome.storage.local. These records remain until the user clears the extension data, deactivates the license where applicable, or uninstalls the extension. Completed MP4 files are saved to the user's chosen Chrome Downloads location.

Sharing and transmission

Video Saver does not send page information, page URLs, media, download history, or diagnostic logs to the developer, licensing service, or payment provider. It has no analytics, advertising, or Video Saver account system. Media requests are made only to the webpage and media hosts needed to retrieve the video selected by the user.

Pro purchases open Stripe Managed Payments' hosted checkout; the extension does not collect card details. Stripe and Link process payment and billing information under their own policies. Activation, validation and deactivation send the license key and installation identifier to Video Saver's licensing service hosted on Cloudflare. Its database stores a hash of the key, Stripe purchase/customer identifiers, product/price IDs, plan, status, expiry, activation identifiers and timestamps for the life of the license. It does not store customer names or email addresses. These records are used only for purchase fulfillment, the three-installation limit, and license support. Rate limiting uses a hashed token derived from the requester's IP address and minute, retained for up to two minutes; application code does not store raw IP addresses. Infrastructure providers may process IP addresses and request metadata under their own policies. If optional license email delivery is enabled, the purchaser email is read transiently from Stripe and sent with the key to Resend for transactional delivery. No browsing or media data is included. Video Saver does not sell personal information.

Remote code and security

Video Saver does not download or execute remote code. All executable JavaScript and the media conversion library are included in the extension package. Remote playlists, media segments, and licensing responses are treated only as data. HTTPS resources remain protected by HTTPS during transmission. The extension does not attempt to bypass DRM and does not access authentication cookies.

User choices

Users initiate downloads themselves and may cancel an active download. Pro users may deactivate the current browser from the Pro page. Removing the extension deletes its local extension data; users should deactivate first if they want to free an installation for another browser.

Limited Use

Information is used only to provide Video Saver's single user-facing video-saving purpose. It is not used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or unrelated purposes.
